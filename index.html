Moonmad influence cheat sheet

Moonmad are bound to the moon and prone to mood swings. Each phase-specific influence applies on the day before, the day of, and the day after its principal phase. Dates outside those three-day windows have no specific lunar urge.

New MoonLethargic and prone to withdrawing.
Waxing HalfMercurial and prone to playing tricks.
Full MoonIrritable and prone to fits of anger.
Waning HalfMysterious and prone to keeping secrets.

Resistance: During an active three-day influence window, spend 1 Luck to resist the moon's urges.

Full moon and Hybrid Form

Day before: irritability and heightened anger responses build throughout the day.

Full moon: transformation into Hybrid Form begins at sundown and lasts until sunrise the following day. In that form, a Moonmad is driven more by instinct and rage than rational thought, with a need to hunt.

Day after: a full day of recovery from the previous night's rage and confusion.

  • Slightly larger and bulkier than the usual human form.
  • Animal features resemble the Moonmad's primary animal form.
  • Natural Weapons apply.
  • +1 to rolls using Brawn, stacking with other bonuses.
  • -3 to rolls using Wits.
  • No ranged weapons, only melee weapons.
  • NPCs run, scream, and raise a ruckus.
  • Strong restraints such as chains or cages can contain the character for the duration.
About the calendar

This version contains authoritative principal phase dates for 2026, matching the game's stated Timeanddate reference for New England. Intermediate labels such as Waxing Crescent and Waning Gibbous are inferred from the principal phases. Moonmad influence windows follow Clock's ruling: day before, day of, and day after each principal phase.

The site uses the America/New_York timezone to decide what "today" means, so players outside Vermont still see the correct in-game local date.
